Experiment 25: Shell Programming
(i) Read a Number and Find Its Square
Program:
#!/bin/bash
echo "Enter a number:"
read n
square=$((n*n))
echo "Square = $square"
Output:
Enter a number: 5
Square = 25
Result:
Successfully calculated square of a number.

(ii) Find the Biggest of Three Numbers
Program:
#!/bin/bash

echo "Enter three numbers:"
read a b c

if [ $a -ge $b ] && [ $a -ge $c ]
then
    echo "$a is largest"
elif [ $b -ge $a ] && [ $b -ge $c ]
then
    echo "$b is largest"
else
    echo "$c is largest"
fi
Output:
Enter three numbers:
10 25 15
25 is largest
Result:
Successfully identified the largest among three numbers.
